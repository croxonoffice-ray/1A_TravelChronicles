About

A private record, kept properly.

1A Travel Collection is a private travel log for people who care about more than just where they went — how they flew, who they were with, and whether it was actually worth it.

Every journey becomes a keepsake: the route, the cabin, the service, the small details that make a flight memorable or forgettable, recorded while they're still fresh.

Your data belongs to you. Journeys are stored under your own account and are never visible to anyone else.
